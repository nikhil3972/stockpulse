SITUATION: DEMAND SPIKE
Demand has jumped well above comparable products. Before pricing, judge whether the spike is likely to last (a trend or seasonal shift) or is a one-off burst:
- Lasting and stock is comfortable: a modest increase captures value while demand is high.
- One-off, or you cannot tell: stay cautious. Hold, or make a very small increase, because customers notice sudden jumps on a product they are actively buying, and it hurts trust.
- Spike plus thin stock: lean slightly higher to slow the sell-through.
Never propose a big jump. Explain whether you treated the spike as lasting or one-off, and why.
